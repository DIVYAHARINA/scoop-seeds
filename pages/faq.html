Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Scoop & Seeds nutrition, ingredients, preparation, and order delivery.

How much protein is in each serving?

Every single serving of Scoop & Seeds premix provides a massive 24.6 grams of high-quality protein. It is carefully balanced with complex carbs from oats, healthy fats from nuts and seeds, and natural flavors.

Is there any added refined sugar?

No! All our premixes are 100% free of added refined sugar. We use natural zero-calorie sweeteners and real ingredients to deliver authentic taste without any sugar crash.

How do I prepare Scoop & Seeds premix?

It takes less than 3 minutes! Simply mix 1 scoop of premix with 150ml of cold or warm milk (or water). Stir well or shake in a shaker cup, and your protein-rich meal is ready to enjoy.

What flavors are available?

We offer 3 signature Indian-inspired flavors: Kulfi Masti (rich & aromatic), Mango Magic (tropical & fruity), and Banana Delight (smooth & creamy). We also offer a 3-pack variety combo!

How long does shipping take?

We dispatch orders within 24 hours. Delivery typically takes 2-4 business days across major cities in India.

How do I place an order?

Simply click "Add to Bag" on any product, open your shopping bag, and click "Order on WhatsApp". Our team will immediately confirm your shipping details and dispatch your order.
